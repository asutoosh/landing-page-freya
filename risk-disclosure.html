3. Leverage Risk

Many forex and CFD products are traded on margin, which means:

  • A small market movement can have a proportionately larger impact on your funds
  • You may be required to deposit additional funds at short notice to maintain your position
  • If you fail to meet margin calls, your position may be liquidated at a loss
  • Leveraged trading can result in losses that exceed your initial deposit

4. Cryptocurrency Specific Risks

In addition to general market risks, cryptocurrency trading involves unique risks:

  • Volatility: Cryptocurrency prices are highly volatile and can move significantly in short periods

  • Liquidity Risk: Some cryptocurrencies may have limited liquidity
